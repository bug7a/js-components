This is a demo shop for easy-pwa.js. Open it with https:// or localhost (Live Server), not by double clicking the file. It is an app on phones and tablets only: on a computer, turn on the phone view (Chrome DevTools > Toggle device toolbar) and reload. Then: DevTools > Application > Manifest / Service workers. Turn off the network (DevTools > Network > Offline) and open a product: the "No internet connection" page is shown.
