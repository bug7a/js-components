## Sending the form to your e-mail

`service/send-form-mail.php` sends the form data as an e-mail with the same style as the forms.
Upload it to your server, write your address to `TO_EMAIL` and write the address of the service to the
`SERVICE_URL` constant of the form page. Nothing else is needed. (See `service/readme.md`)
